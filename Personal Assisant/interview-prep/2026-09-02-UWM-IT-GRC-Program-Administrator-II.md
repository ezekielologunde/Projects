### Framework probes — crisp definitions to have ready
- **NIST CSF:** org-level risk framework; functions are Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. Good for structuring a program and measuring maturity.
- **NIST 800‑53 / RMF:** control catalog + the lifecycle — categorize, select, implement, assess, authorize, monitor.
- **ISO 27001:** ISMS standard; risk treatment plan and Statement of Applicability; certified by external audit.
- **SOC 2:** AICPA Trust Services Criteria (security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy). Type I = design at a point in time; Type II = operating effectiveness over a period. Comes up in vendor reviews.
- **Risk register:** risks with likelihood, impact, owner, treatment (accept / mitigate / transfer / avoid), residual risk, review date.
- **Control testing:** sample-based checks that a control actually operated, plus collecting artifacts (tickets, configs, logs, approvals) for auditors.
- **Policy lifecycle:** draft → review → approve → publish → attest → annual review → exception handling.
- **Third-party risk:** intake questionnaire → tier by data sensitivity/criticality → review SOC 2 / pen test → contractual security terms → periodic reassessment.
- **KRIs / metrics:** patch-SLA compliance %, overdue/aging findings, control coverage, open exceptions and their age, phishing failure rate.
- **Mortgage-sector regs (know the names, don't overclaim):** GLBA Safeguards Rule, CFPB/FTC oversight, SOX (public company), state privacy laws, NYDFS 500 if NY-licensed. Say: *"I don't have direct mortgage-sector regulatory experience, but I've worked in regulated environments — HIPAA-aligned public health insurance, and CMMC/800‑171 defense-adjacent — and mapping a regulation to controls and evidence is the same skill."*
